Quick answers

Before You Message Us

A few things travellers often ask. These might answer your question right away.

Gorilla permits

Permits are limited, so we recommend booking a few months ahead, especially for June–September.

Best time to visit

The dry seasons (June–September and December–February) are most popular, but Uganda is great all year.

Custom trips

Every tour can be changed. Add days, swap parks or upgrade lodges. Just tell us what you'd like.

Visas

Most visitors apply online before travelling. A yellow fever certificate is required on arrival.

Our story

A small team with a big love for the wild

Gratius Tours began in 2015 as a small group of guides who were passionate about showing guests Uganda's wild places, the way locals experience them.

Today we plan private safaris across the country, from gorilla trekking in Bwindi and chimpanzee tracking in Kibale to game drives in Queen Elizabeth, boat trips on the Nile and cultural visits with local communities. Every trip is built around our guests: their dates, their interests and their pace.

Responsible travel

Travel That Gives Back

Every safari supports the wildlife you came to see and the communities who live alongside it.

Protecting wildlife

Park and permit fees help fund conservation and the rangers who protect gorillas and other animals.

Supporting communities

We work with local guides, drivers, lodges and craftspeople so tourism income stays in Uganda.

Respecting culture

Cultural visits are run with and for the communities, never as a show.

Leaving no trace

We follow park rules, keep a respectful distance from wildlife and reduce waste on every trip.

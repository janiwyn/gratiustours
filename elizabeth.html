3 DAY QUEEN ELIZABETH NATIONAL PARK SAFARI AND KAZINGA CHANNEL

PERIOD – 3DAYS

This tour takes you to Uganda's second biggest wildlife park, Queen Elizabeth. Along the way, you’ll cross the equator and enjoy sightings of zebras in Lake Mburo National Park. During a game drive, you'll have the chance to see buffaloes, hippos, crocodiles, lions, hyenas, and many more wildlife species. One the second day you’ll go on a fantastic boat ride on the Kazinga Channel, which offers stunning views of Lake George and Lake Edward.

Day by Day Itinerary

Arrival

    Arrival & Airport Pickup

    Arrival at Entebbe, meet your guide and transfer to your hotel.

    Main Destination: Entebbe (City)
    • Meals & Drinks: Breakfast included (Lunch & Dinner not included). Drinking water provided.
    Day 2

    Queen Elizabeth NP

    Pick up and Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park

    After your early morning breakfast, you will be picked by an Otter African Safaris guide and start your journey to Queen Elizabeth National Park in Kasese in Western Uganda. First stop will be at the Equator in Kayabwe for memorable photos and the famous water experiment that shows that earth is separated into the Northern and Southern Hemisphere and you will have a chance to enjoy both the hemispheres. A lunch break will be in Mbarara at Igongo Cultural Centre and after you will continue your journey to Queen Elizabeth National Park.

    Best Time & Wildlife:
    Hippo: Abundant
    Chimpanzee: Occasional
    Elephant: Common
    White Rhino: Common
    Lion: Common
    Leopard: Occasional
    Accommodation & Meals:

    ≯ Mid-range lodge located just outside Queen Elizabeth NP

    Day 3

    Kazinga Channel & Return

    Morning Game Drive and Afternoon boat trip on Kazinga Channel

    Have your breakfast very early in the morning, depart for an early morning game drive as early as 6am to the rolling plains of Uganda’s second largest National Park and look out for lions, leopards, Jackson hartebeest, buffaloes, hippos, waterbucks, warthogs, leopards, Uganda Kobs, oribis, and elephants. For any birder, there are quite a number of birds to sight too during the drive. Return to the lodge for some rest time and lunch. After lunch at the lodge, you will go for a 2-hour boat cruise trip on the Kazinga Channel which connects Lakes George and Edward and you will surely be fascinated by the concentrations of giant hippos, buffaloes, elephants and crocodiles among others. You will also see many varieties of birds at the water shores. You will have dinner and overnight at the lodge.

    Main Destination:

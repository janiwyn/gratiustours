Good to know

What's Usually Included

Each tour page lists its exact details. Here's what most of our packages cover.

Included
  • Airport pickup and drop-off at Entebbe
  • Private 4x4 safari vehicle with fuel
  • English-speaking driver-guide
  • Park entry fees (for non-residents)
  • Gorilla or chimp permits on primate tours
  • Accommodation and meals as listed
  • Activities in the itinerary (game drives, boat cruises)
  • Drinking water in the vehicle
Not included
  • International flights
  • Visa fees and travel insurance
  • Tips for guides and porters
  • Optional activities not in the itinerary
  • Drinks and personal items (souvenirs, laundry)
  • Government increases in taxes or park fees
Want something changed? Every package can be adjusted. Just tell us in your booking request.

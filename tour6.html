2 DAY MURCHISON FALLS AND WILDLIFE GAME VIEWING

PERIOD – 2DAYS

    Day 2

    Murchison Falls

    Transfer to Murchison Falls N/Park

    At 6:00am, we will begin our drive to Murchison Falls National Park, which takes approximately 5 to 6 hours, depending on traffic. Along the way, we will stop for lunch before continuing our journey. Upon arrival, we will be welcomed by a troop of baboons as we enter the park. We will visit the top of the falls. Murchison Falls is known for being one of the most powerful waterfalls in the world. Water cascades through a gorge that measures 8m (26ft) wide. After visiting the falls, we shall do a boat ride and then we will embark on a short evening game drive through the park before heading to the lodge for dinner and an overnight stay

    Main Destination : Murchison Falls National Park

    Best Time To Visit December to February (Easier to spot animals)

    High Season June to September (Peak time for Uganda)

    Best Weather June and July (Less rain and lower temperatures)

    Return to Entebbe

    Morning Game drive and transfer to the airport At 5:30am, we will enjoy coffee or tea before departing for an early game drive with a packed breakfast. During this drive, we will have the chance to see animals such as hyenas, lions, leopards and buffalo. We will stop at the famous hippo pool to enjoy our packed breakfast. After the break, we will continue our game drive and after our activities in the park, we will drive out and head to the ziwa rhion sanctuary for a 1 hour trek before heading to Kampala or Entebbe, arriving at your lodge between 6:00pm and 7:00pm.
